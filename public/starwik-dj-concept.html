01 — THE ARTIST

Satwik.
Known as ★wik.

DEEP HOUSE · MELODIC HOUSE · AFRO HOUSE

Music was always in me — songs, lyrics, compositions. A language I understood before I understood why. When house music found me, something cracked open. I didn't plan to become a DJ. I just followed the feeling and couldn't stop.

I call myself an experience curator because the music is only part of it. The other part is the room — reading it, feeling every shift, pulling people into a vibe before they even realise they're in it. I don't just play tracks. I curate a journey. Every set is a conversation between me and the floor.
